# Notifications

The bell icon in the top bar gives you live updates on background work you
triggered — exports, imports, and similar long-running actions — delivered
in real time rather than requiring a page refresh.

- Notifications arrive as toast pop-ups as they happen (up to 3 shown at
  once; more are queued).
- The notification panel (opened from the bell icon) keeps a running list
  you can review, open for detail, or clear.
- Typical triggers include: an export finishing and becoming ready to
  download, a bulk import completing (or failing validation), and other
  asynchronous jobs you've started elsewhere in the app.
