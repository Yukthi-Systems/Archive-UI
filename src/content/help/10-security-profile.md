# Security & Profile

## Profile (Settings → Profile)

Update your own account details: **Username**, **Display Name**,
**Email Address**, and **Phone Number**. Only the fields you change are
submitted when you click **Save Changes**.

This is different from the read-only **Profile** summary page reachable
from the top-bar avatar menu, which shows your account and permission
summary without an edit form.

## Security (Settings → Security)

| Feature | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Change Password** | Update your login password. |
| **Authenticator App (TOTP)** | Enable/disable app-based codes; manage the list of active authenticator devices. |
| **SMS Verification** | Enable/disable SMS one-time codes. Requires a phone number on your account. |
| **Email Verification** | Enable/disable email-based one-time codes. Requires an email address on your account. |

Every password change and 2FA change here is recorded in the Audit Logs.
