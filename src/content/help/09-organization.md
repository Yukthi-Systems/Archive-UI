# Organization Settings

Manage your organization's primary contact information, used for system
alerts such as quota warnings.

| Field | Rules |
|---|---|
| **Admin Email** | Required, standard email format. |
| **Admin Phone** | Required, E.164 format with country code (e.g. `+919845061219`). |

Click **Save Changes** to apply the update.
